# Brag Plan: Apartment Manager

## What is this app?
A shared app where housemates tap "I did X" for chores, an admin approves it, and a leaderboard ranks who actually keeps the apartment alive.

## The angle
Chore wars as a sports broadcast. The dishes become a competitive sport, the admin is the referee, the leaderboard is the scoreboard. The joke is the PRD's own absurd specificity: "Forgot to wash cup -1", "Toilet champion", and an admin who is explicitly not allowed to be scored.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant display type: "Who cleaned the toilet?" Beat. A single housemate taps "Cleaned toilet +5". The question is the hook; the tap is the answer.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The claim: a catalog card "Cleaned toilet +5" gets tapped, turns into a pending claim.
- The referee: admin inbox row, cursor hits Accept, a +5 flies onto the board.
- The leaderboard: Sam 42, Alex 31, Jo 18. Sam's row is lime and takes the top; points count up.
- The medals: "Toilet champion" and a 4-week streak chip land on Sam's row, one by one.

## Outro / punchline
Admin line: "Admins are not scored." Then logo + "Apartment Manager. Settle it with points."

## User flow worth showing
1. Housemate taps a deed from the catalog ("Cleaned toilet +5").
2. Admin sees the pending claim and accepts.
3. Leaderboard updates; medals appear.
(Schedule and budget are not live, so they are not shown.)

- Preset: default
- Creative direction: sports broadcast for chores
- Interpretation: playful and clean; quick cuts with proper holds, deadpan scoreboard captions, no mess.

## Duration: 20s

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, upbeat, around 120 BPM
- Music treatment: start at 0s, low bed under hook, lift for the leaderboard, fade over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Tempo ~120.19 BPM. Strong cues in the window at 16.02, 17.02, 18.02, 20.02s; beat grid 3.02-24.52s at 0.5s steps. Target the leaderboard reveal near 8.0s and the logo near 16.0s on the nearest beat; ignore cues if they hurt readability.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass lightly pulses the lime row glow.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (tap, accept, count-up ticks, medal pop)
- Audio-coupled moments: the tap on the deed card, the Accept click, the points count-up, medals landing one by one
- Restraint rule: no stinger spam; nothing over the hook line.

## Storyboard

Music: upbeat bed enters.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The claim and the referee — 5s

Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor clicks Accept.
Audio intent: satisfying approval.
Audio-coupled idea: accept click then soft success tick.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The leaderboard — 6s
"This week" leaderboard: Sam 42 (lime, #1), Alex 31, Jo 18. Sam's total counts from 37 to 42 as the +5 lands. Rows arrive one by one, hold 0.8s each once settled.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three rows arrive in order, then the count-up.
Audio intent: scoreboard energy.
Audio-coupled idea: count-up ticks on the beat grid.
Music: lift.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Medals — 3s
Chips land on Sam's row one by one: "Toilet champion", "4-week streak". Caption: "Awarded automatically."
Sequential/interaction: yes — two chips, ~1s apart.
Audio intent: small proud pops.
Audio-coupled idea: medal pop per chip.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

Text: "Admins are not scored." (held ~1.2s), then logo lockup "Apartment Manager" with the line "Settle it with points."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: warm landing.
Audio-coupled idea: single logo hit on a strong beat.
Music: fade out over the last 1.5s.
